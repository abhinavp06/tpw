It's been quite a while since I wrote my last blog. I tried writing again in this span of time, however, I just wasn't able to.
<br/><br/>
Who I am now is very different from who I was since I wrote my last blog. A lot of experiences, new memories, a new direction, a new person altogether. I seem to have made progress in my spiritual journey (I'd like to think so).
<br/><br/>
After my last blog, I realised that I had become much more self-aware. I noticed that I had started observing anything and everything. Trying to find the "oneness" and also just playing around with psychology. Making assumptions about how the person I am talking to talks that way, why they sit or stand in a certain manner, what things could have been factors of their masculinity or femininity.
How the concept of consciousness, thought, Brahman, God and other things come into play even in a simple conversation or even just me thinking of something. Sometimes when I think, I end up trying to observe thought's movement instead of actually thinking.
But you see, this has unfortunately caused a problem - I am not able to actually be in the moment and listen to what they are actually telling me or what I was even thinking about. It's like the content of the conversation or the thought does not matter that much. It's just that I am too interested in what is going on inside their mind or my mind subconsciously instead of actually participating in the act. I am too unattached to any experience because the mental games help me travel into another entity.
<br/><br/>
_Is this the tragedy of self-awareness?_
<br/><br/>
But how is it that I am able to pierce into their mind, their childhood, their life so easily while also being ME. How am I able to travel into their own experiences, their own memory, visualize things when I actually am right here? How is it possible that who I am now as I write this is so different compared to who I am when I am conversing with someone? Even if it was 5 minutes ago?
<br/><br/>
_Am I a quantum being?_
<br/><br/>
But if this is the case, then who is it who is actually moving? Who is the one actually having these experiences? Who is the one who visualizes? Who is the one who thinks? Who is the one who sees this thinking? How am I different from my observation and state of mind when I clearly established in the last blog that _the observer is the observed_? Is it possible that this is nothing but another thought? That there is no <b>"enlightenment"</b>? Who is getting enlightened?
<br/><br/>
_Is this just another trick by the ego?_
